# Limitations

Disabling is not uninstalling. Per-user removal is not removal of a factory APEX. An active APEX is not necessarily a running process, and an idle observed connection is not evidence of universal network silence. Different observation windows cannot establish a before/after causal improvement without controlled conditions.

Root and kernel modifications alone do not supply a qualified signature-compatibility policy. A second profile does not solve the same-package/different-certificate replacement problem. General microG installation guidance is not a tested Nothing OS migration procedure. Application, notification, banking and Play Integrity compatibility were not tested under microG. OTA persistence, complete recovery without data loss, hardware recovery and data-backup restoration were not qualified.

Properties reporting locked/green in a modified system are not proof that the physical bootloader is locked. No firmware, APEX, keys, personal applications or device identifiers are distributed. Recheck all conclusions after a firmware update.
